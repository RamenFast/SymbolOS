# Feeling and form

SymbolOS uses **Fi** for felt meaning and **Ti** for clear structure. These are writing lenses here: keep what matters to someone beside what is defined, bounded and checkable.

A symbol can carry tone and help recall a concept. It should not make the reader guess a requirement.

## The two-line mirror

- **Heart:** “I want this tool to feel calm when I return after a long break.”
- **Mind:** “Show the current task, the last verified state and one next action before the history.”

The second line gives the first something you can build and check. Neither line needs to impersonate a sensor or an emotional diagnosis.

## Return to the reason

When the design gets noisy, ask:

- What matters to the person using it?
- What are we trying to make legible?
- What did we observe, and what are we assuming?
- What is the smallest next step that serves the purpose?

This is the useful part of returning to the meeting place: reconnect a local decision to the reason for the project, then come back with something concrete.

## A small shared vocabulary

- ☂️ **Umbrella:** care, consent and ownership. Not a privacy setting.
- 🧬 **Meeting place:** the shared context and the work in front of us.
- 🗺️ **Map:** make relationships understandable.
- 🧠 **Mind:** definitions, reasoning and structure.
- ❤️ **Heart:** values and expressed priorities.
- 🪞 **Mirror:** notice the gap between an assumption and an observation.
